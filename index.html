Questions

Does it really work on servers?

Yes. Anti-Cheat hides blocks and chunks, but the server still has to send certain information to the client. Our finders use that information to detect what’s happening around you.

How fast do I get it?

Straight away. Your key comes as soon as the payment clears, and you download from the dashboard.

I got a new PC. Now what?

Reset your HWID directly in the dashboard! You get two resets per month, and they refill on the 1st of every month. If you ever have an emergency or need some extra help, feel free to open a support ticket! Our AI assistant and staff are always here to help you out.

Which Minecraft version?

Java 1.21.11 on Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or newer, with Fabric API and Java 21. More versions are coming soon!

Anything for DonutSMP?

Made specifically for DonutSMP. Check the Modules tab to see everything included.
